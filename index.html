CYBERSECURITY IN 2025: STAY SAFE

AI is in the hands of good people and bad people. Trust is the skill.

Daily article — November 5, 2026  |  Silent Security Vault — Domenick Arlon Hall, Mobile, AL

Cybersecurity is the work of keeping bad people out of your computer, your phone, and your accounts. In 2025, the research firms said the same thing: digital trust and cybersecurity are the foundation everything else stands on — because AI is now in the hands of attackers too.

What you need to know

  • AI makes scams sound real. A fake email or a fake voice can now sound like a real person. If a message rushes you, stop. Real companies do not rush you.
  • Your password is still the front door. Use a different password for email than for everything else. Turn on the extra code (two-step) on your email first.
  • Updates close the holes. When your phone or computer offers an update, take it. Most break-ins use holes that were already fixed — by people who did not update.
  • Back it up. A backup is your way back if something goes wrong. Copy your photos and important files somewhere else, not just on the one computer.
  • When in doubt, ask a person. If a message says your account is locked, your money is at risk, or you won something — do not click. Call me or the real company first.

What to do next

If you got a message that felt wrong, do not delete it yet. Show it to me and I will help you check if it is real.
